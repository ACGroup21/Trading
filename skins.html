Skin options

Six directions for the Trading Ladder Tracker. Every card below is the same markup — tiles, buttons, a preview, two deviation flags and a multiplier chart. Only the sixteen CSS custom properties change, which is all a skin is.

What to judge them on. In this app colour carries meaning: good, warn and critical tell you whether a trade hit, drifted or cost you. So the accent that runs the interface has to stay clearly apart from those three — otherwise every bar on the chart reads as a win. Each card ends with whether it holds that line.
Phosphoramber CRT The Matrix idea with the green taken out — amber monochrome, which is what those terminals actually were. Mono throughout, sharp corners.
