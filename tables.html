Table options

Six treatments of the same six trades. The first is what the tracker does today; the rest are alternatives, two of which change the structure rather than the paint.

The diagnosis first. The styling isn't really what's wrong. Your entries table carries 12 columns and the projection 10, both forced to min-width:900px, every figure right-aligned at identical weight with a rule under every row. That is a wall of digits with nothing leading. Restyling makes a dense table handsomer — it is still dense. C, D and E below attack the column count; A, B and F only attack the look.

Grid rules on every row, all nine columns at equal weight, 900px minimum so it scrolls sideways on anything but a wide screen.
