How the split works. When you close a trade, a share of the total you finished with is moved to safe haven and never traded again; the rest rolls into your next trade. You set that share on each trade, alongside how much of the pot you stake in the first place. Banking less grows the pot faster; banking more locks away more. Both are saved with each trade, so changing them later never rewrites your history.

Where your data lives. In this browser, on this machine. Nothing is ever sent anywhere — there is no account and no server. Storage belongs to the site, not to a file, so every page here shares one set of entries and keeps them between visits. A copy opened from your own disk is a separate island with its own empty tracker, which is worth knowing before you save one. Use Download my data for a backup or to move between machines, and Save this as… to keep more than one plan side by side.

This is a calculator, not financial advice — I'm not a financial adviser. It works out the arithmetic of the plan you gave it; whether the plan is achievable is a separate question, and a long run of trades all reaching their target is a very demanding assumption. The odds figure in the Projection is only ever as good as the hit rate you feed it.
